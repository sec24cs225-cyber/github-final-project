# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or nationality.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and other community interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly, and appropriate action will be taken.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may follow these guidelines:
1. Correction: A private or public warning about inappropriate behavior.
2. Warning: A formal warning for repeated or serious violations.
3. Temporary Ban: Temporary restriction from project participation.
4. Permanent Ban: Permanent removal from the project community for serious or repeated violations.
